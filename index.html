How to compete.

01

100M SPRINT

Wait for the gun, then alternate A / D or the left/right pads. A fast, clean rhythm builds speed. A tap before the gun is a false start.

02

LONG JUMP

Your run-up is automatic. Tap SPACE as you cross the glowing takeoff zone, then tap again at the top of the flight to extend your landing.

03

JAVELIN

Release the spear as close to the foul line as you dare. Time your tap so the arm angle is in the gold band near 36° for maximum distance.
